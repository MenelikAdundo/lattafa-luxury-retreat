Booking, payment & cancellation policy

Our policy is designed to keep bookings flexible for guests while giving the retreat a fair chance to prepare every stay.

How payments work

  • Pay in full online: Guests can pay the full quoted accommodation amount securely by M-Pesa or card when online checkout is available.
  • 30% deposit: A 30% payment secures the dates. The remaining 70% is paid at check-in.
  • Pay at check-in: Standard room requests may be held for up to 12 hours while availability is confirmed. Payment is then due in full at check-in.
  • Groups and private events: Whole-compound and private-event reservations require a 50% deposit to secure dates.

Cancellations and changes

  • Cancel 7 or more days before check-in for a full refund of accommodation payments, less any non-refundable payment-provider charges.
  • Cancel 3–6 days before check-in for a 50% refund of accommodation payments.
  • Within 72 hours of check-in, deposits and accommodation payments are non-refundable.
  • Guests who do not arrive for a confirmed booking are not eligible for a refund.
  • Where possible, date changes are accommodated without charge when requested at least 7 days before arrival and subject to availability.

Confirmation and arrival

  • A booking is confirmed only when the Lattafa team sends a confirmation message or email.
  • Guests should present a valid booking confirmation on arrival.
  • Please share your expected arrival time in advance, especially for late arrivals or group stays.
